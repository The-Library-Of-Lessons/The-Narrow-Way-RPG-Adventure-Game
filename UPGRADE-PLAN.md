# Nine Roads — spoiler-free development plan

## Design commitments

Build on all five existing regions and add a crowded harbor, a divided hillside town, a winter refuge, and a storm-threatened crossing. The thematic center is Jesus' two greatest commandments: love God and love your neighbor (Matthew 22:37–40). Fictional choices must not be presented as additional commands from God.

The pivotal scene is intentionally omitted from this document. It will involve two legitimate goods, a meaningful irreversible cost, informed consent, time to think, and two complete hopeful resolutions. Reflexes, difficulty settings, or obscure collectibles must never determine an irreversible story outcome.

## Implementation sequence

1. Keyboard choices: arrows cycle; Enter selects; visible selection and focus; no accidental double-selection.
2. Existing-region polish: observation clues, progressive hints, visible restoration, distinct NPC appearances and activity animation, companion scenes, persistent consequences.
3. Navigation and mobile: optional objective guidance, north indicator and legible map markers, context-specific interaction verbs, adjustable touch controls.
4. Four new authored regions: distinct geometry, landmarks, ambient themes, environmental mechanisms, conversations and practical service.
5. Relational continuity: earlier choices alter later assistance, dialogue, and epilogue details without creating a hidden moral score.
6. Finale: preparedness, deliberation, explicit confirmation, playable follow-through, quiet aftermath, and an epilogue that remembers people and places.
7. Save migration and verification: retain existing progress; persist puzzles, choices and checkpoints; test both resolutions and keyboard-only selection; inspect desktop and phone layouts.

## Pacing target

Target at least two hours for a first-time player exploring, reading, reflecting and completing optional community stories. Do not pad the clock with forced waits, repeated gathering or long walks. Completion-time measurements from human playtests are required before claiming this duration. Automated completion proves reachability, not two hours of satisfying play.

## Release gates

Implementation status: nine regions, progressive hints, restoration effects, distinct NPC palettes and interaction gestures, contextual controls with size settings, companion dialogue, twelve community requests, two final branches, and pre-decision export are implemented. Expansion integration and full keyboard-walking tests pass, including both resolutions; the original prologue, five-region campaign, and checkpoint regressions also pass. This is a substantial playable prototype, not a finished commercial release. Human pacing, emotional-impact review, and real Android testing remain open gates.

- All nine regions can be entered and revisited where the story permits.
- Every required puzzle offers a discoverable clue and escalating assistance.
- All six improvement categories have working in-game changes.
- Both final resolutions are complete and saveable; an exported pre-decision save enables replay.
- The finale cannot be triggered by a stray keypress or a missed combat action.
- Prior saves resume without losing progress.
- Fresh-player pacing, emotional response and real Android touch testing remain separate human validation tasks.
